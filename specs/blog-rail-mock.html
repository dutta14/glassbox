Glassbox rail · B “Archive” · deep mock

Static HTML. Tokens from the app. Italic titles are verse. Nothing here is wired to the real app.

1 · Live frame

Try it: click a note, click a year to fold it, hover a row and open its ⋯ menu, switch between Posts and Collections (open a collection to see its page), click Search (the tabs step aside) and type (“the”, “rain”, “zzz”), click New, or fold the rail with the button by the masthead. The rail takes the page background and has no border; only whitespace separates it from the writing.

2 · Row states

No filled pill anywhere. The only signals are the title colour and the ◇. Titles wrap rather than truncate, so nothing is ever cut mid-word. The ⋯ column is reserved so the wrap point never moves on hover.

3 · Year headings, menus and edge cases

4 · Posts and Collections tabs

Posts lists every post that is not in a collection, grouped by year, newest first. Collections lists the collections themselves, one row each with a chapter count: no years, no posts underneath. A collection has its own identity, like a book on a shelf, so its chapters live on its own page in the main pane. A post in a collection never appears under Posts, so each post lives in exactly one place. The Organize menu goes away, and the tabs never change what the main pane shows.

5 · Reading and writing

The middle pane is one thing at a time: the feed, or the editor. New and each post’s Edit swap the feed for the editor you have today (prose or verse). Done or Esc swaps back and lands on the same post, so the rail marker and the page always agree. New always opens in Posts and closes search; a collection is chosen afterwards from the ⋯ menu. Try it in the live frame above.

  1. New ▾ → Prose or Verse. Search closes, the tab switches to Posts, a draft titled “Untitled” appears at the top of the current year, marked as the open note. Focus is in the title.
  2. Writing. The title edits the rail row as you type. Everything autosaves; there is no Save button.
  3. Done. The feed returns with the new post at the very top, the rail marker on it. A draft with no title and no text is discarded instead of saved.
  4. Edit on an existing post. Same swap, focus in the body. Done returns to that post wherever it sits in the feed.
  5. Clicking another row while writing saves, then scrolls the feed to that row.

6 · Post header and search results

A post in a collection says so in one quiet line under the title. It used to sit 8px from the title; it now has room to breathe. The same line shows in the feed, in search results and in the editor, and it links to that collection’s page, scrolled to this chapter. While searching, matches are marked wherever they appear: in the rail row, in the feed’s title and body.

7 · Collapsed rail and small screens

Collapsing removes the rail entirely. The page keeps the full width and a single menu button stays at the top left; it brings the rail back. Under 900px the same button opens the rail as a drawer over a dimmed page.

Reading feedThe middle pane shows posts that are not in a collection, newest first, as a continuous feed; or, while searching, a flat feed of every match. It does not follow the tab: switching Posts / Collections never changes the pane. Four posts load at first; four more load when a sentinel comes within 400px of the bottom, with a 450ms “Loading more…” state, ending in “That’s everything.” Posts are separated by 140px and an ornament. The first post never touches the top edge: the pane has 72px of top padding (56px under 900px), so the title starts well below the window edge and lines up with the masthead’s breathing room; the same padding applies above the editor and a collection page. It scrolls away with the content. Each is an <article aria-labelledby>; the pane is <main>. Clicking a rail row scrolls the feed there (smooth, instant under reduced motion) and loads forward as needed. The open post is part of the route, not the hash (the app uses a HashRouter), and the route is replaced, not pushed, as you read. Search resets the feed to the top. A long library should window or unload posts far from view; the verse measuring runs only in the editor, so it never runs per feed post.
WritingNew and Edit replace the main pane with the existing editor; Done or Esc returns to the same post (or, when editing a chapter from a collection page, to that collection at that chapter). New always switches to Posts, closes search and opens a draft at the top of the current year. Autosave, no Save button; an empty new draft is discarded on close. Choosing a collection happens afterwards from the ⋯ menu. Starting another action while writing saves first. Out of the app: nothing is created until the first keystroke, so New then Done leaves no trace.
Selected markerFollows the post being read: as you scroll, the ◇ and full ink move to the post crossing the reading band (25% down the pane), and the rail scrolls itself to keep the row in view. In the editor it marks the note being written. Filled 6px ◇ in --color-text at x=3px. Hover shows the same ◇ hollow at 55%. Verse titles are italic everywhere in the rail.

FocusExisting --shadow-focus ring around the row link or heading, 4px radius. Search input has no ring; the underline thickens instead.
Motion150ms colour / opacity, 150ms chevron turn. All off under prefers-reduced-motion.
GroupingPosts tab: by createdAt year, newest first, all open by default, fold state per session. A year holding the selected note or a search match is forced open. Invalid dates fall into a final “Undated” group.
TabsPosts and Collections under the action links. Garamond 16px, no rule or underline; the open tab is --color-text, the other --color-text-faint. role="tablist", arrow keys move between tabs, the list is the tabpanel. The open tab is remembered per device. While searching the tabs are hidden (see Search) and return when search closes.
Posts / CollectionsPosts: notes without a collection, grouped by year, then “New” lives in the action links. Collections: a flat list of collections, one row each: the name (Garamond 14px, same row as a post) and beneath it “N chapters · YYYY–YYYY” (Inter 11px, uppercase, 0.08em, faint; “1 chapter”; the years are the first and last year in the chapters’ written dates, a single year reads “2011”, never “2011–2011”; “Empty”, with no years, at zero). No year headings, no collection headings, no fold, no posts underneath. Ordered by end year, newest first: the last year in the collection’s “YYYY–YYYY” decides, ties broken by the latest written date. Collections with no chapters yet sit above the rest (newest first), so a collection you just created is where you left it. The open collection takes the filled ◇ and full ink, as a post does. Below the list: “New collection”. No Organize menu. A collection’s posts never show under Posts. The existing BookListItem is this row.
Collection pageOpens in the main pane when a collection is chosen in the rail, or its line is followed from a post. Title (Garamond 44px), “N chapters · YYYY–YYYY” beneath (Inter 12px, uppercase; same years rule as the rail row), the ornament, then every chapter as an article in the collection’s own running order (the order chosen on the page, not by date). No infinite scroll and no loader: all chapters render, as BookStage does today. Ends with “End of Name.” Each chapter ends with a quiet line: its written date, then “Edit” and “Remove from collection” (Inter 11px, uppercase, 0.08em, faint; the date is “MONTH D, YYYY”; no “Written” and no read time here). These are the two actions the app has today. Done returns to the page at that chapter. Rename and reorder stay as in BookStage. Empty: title, “Empty”, “No chapters yet.” The mock orders chapters by date, oldest first, as a stand-in for the running order.
SearchInline, not a modal. “Search” swaps the New / Search links for a field in the same spot (Garamond italic 17px, underline only, no box). While it is open the Posts / Collections tabs are hidden and the list becomes one flat set of results from every post and every collection, newest first, with no year or collection headings. Each row’s date line carries the year and the collection name. A small count (“3 results”) sits above the list. Matches title, body and tags, as the modal does today. A match wash marks the title hit; a body-only match shows one muted line of text under the date (this mock filters titles only, so the snippet is not shown). Empty field: “Search every post and collection.” No results: “Nothing matches “zzz”.” Esc or the “esc” button closes search, restores the tabs, the previous tab and fold state. Field: role="searchbox" labelled “Search notes”; the count is a polite live region. No scrim, no focus trap, so the page stays readable and clickable.
Collection lineShown on any post that is in a collection: in the feed (search results only, since the Posts feed holds no collection posts), on the collection page chapters’ editor, and in the editor. Copy: “In the collection – Name” (the real app says “In the Book - ” today; the hyphen becomes an en dash). Inter 12px / 1.6, 0.08em tracking, uppercase, --color-text-muted; the name is a link underlined at rest, 4px offset, --color-border-strong, full ink on hover and focus. Spacing: 20px under the title’s last line (the old −8px pulled it into the title), then the ornament 22px below, measured from the line’s box. Centred, max 30em, wraps on long names (overflow-wrap: anywhere). An empty name reads “Untitled collection”. Not shown for a post outside a collection. The link goes to that collection’s page, scrolled to this chapter (the existing /book/:id route with a chapter target); the rail switches to Collections and marks the collection. From the editor it saves first.
Search highlightingSearch matches title, body and tags, case-insensitive, as a substring. Every occurrence is marked with <mark> (--color-raised wash, full ink, 2px radius, 1px side padding), never a bright colour. Marked in three places: the rail row’s title; the rail row’s body snippet (shown only when the title has no hit: one line of text around the first hit, about 40 characters before, clipped to two lines, with … at a cut end); and, in the feed, each result’s title and body. Marks are cleared when search closes or the query is emptied. The feed scrolls to the top of the first result, not to a hit inside it. No “next match” control in this iteration. Screen readers hear the text unchanged (mark is not announced as a change).
Row ⋯ menu actionsPost rows. Turn into prose / verse: the post re-renders in place in the feed and the rail title goes italic or upright; nothing moves. Add to collection: opens a small list of collections (plus “New collection”); on choosing, the post leaves Posts, joins that collection as its last chapter and the feed and marker advance to the next post below (the one above if it was last). Delete: keeps today’s confirmation; afterwards the marker and feed move to the next post below, or the one above, or to the empty state if none remain. Collection rows. Delete collection only, with the existing confirmation. The posts are kept and return to Posts in their year. Rename, reorder and “Remove from collection” live on the collection’s page, where the chapters are. A collection whose last chapter is removed stays, “Empty”, until deleted.
New collectionThe link turns into a one-line name field (Garamond italic 17px, underline only, same as search). Enter creates an empty collection and opens its page; Esc cancels; an empty name is ignored. An empty collection shows “Empty” in the rail and, on its page, the title, “Empty” and “No chapters yet. Add a post from its ⋯ menu.”
Feed & rail agreementOne invariant: the marked row is the thing in the main pane. On Posts that is the post in the reading band, or the note in the editor; it changes on scroll, on clicking a row, on Done, on New, and on search. On Collections it is the open collection, and the pane shows its page. Following a collection line opens the page and switches the rail to Collections. If the marked post is in a folded year, that heading is forced open. If a post is not yet loaded, clicking its row loads forward to it, then scrolls. If the list changes under the reader (search), the feed resets and the marker goes to the first post, except closing search, which keeps what was open before.
Deep link & reloadThe URL hash is the open post’s id. Opening a URL with a hash loads forward to that post and scrolls to it; an unknown or deleted id falls back to the first post and drops the hash. Back and Forward do not step through every post scrolled past (replaceState). The open tab is remembered per device, so a hash for a post in the other tab switches tabs.
Empty and first-run statesNo notes: rail shows “Nothing here yet. Start with the first line.”, the feed shows the same centred in the page and New stays available. Sample notes banner above the first year until deleted. No collections: the Collections tab shows its empty message and “New collection”. An empty collection’s page: its title, “Empty”, “No chapters yet.” Search with an empty field: “Search every post and collection.” No results: the feed says “Nothing to show.”
KeyboardTab order: masthead, collapse, New, Search, tabs, headings and rows, then the page. Enter on a row scrolls the feed and keeps focus on the row. Each post’s Edit link is reachable by Tab in the feed. In the editor: Esc or the Done button returns to the feed. In search: Esc closes. Left and Right switch tabs. The feed pane is tabindex="-1" so it can take programmatic focus after Done.
Small screensUnder 900px the rail is a drawer. Choosing a row closes the drawer and scrolls the feed to that post. The feed runs full width with 20px side padding, a 96px gap between posts, and titles at 32px. The collection line and Edit link wrap rather than truncate.
Dark modeEvery colour above is a token, so nothing is hard-coded. Marks use --color-raised, which stays visible on the dark page. Ornaments and rules use the same tokens as today.
PerformanceThe feed renders four posts, then four per page. Past about 60 loaded posts, posts far above the viewport are replaced by fixed-height spacers so scroll position holds. Scroll-spy uses one IntersectionObserver, never scroll events. Search runs on the client over all notes; results render in the same pages of four.
Removed from the appThe search modal (NoteSearchModal, with its dialog, backdrop and focus trap), the Organize menu and its kebab trigger (OrganizeMenu), the “In one list / By book” options and their copy (organizeOptionList, organizeOptionBook), and the OrganizeMode preference (useNoteOrganize, key glassbox.notesOrganize.v1). The rail’s separate Notes and Books sections and the Books entry go too; the Posts / Collections tabs replace them. Unchanged: BookListItem (the collection row) and BookStage (the collection page, with its contents, rename and reorder). Tests that assume the “list” default or the book-grouped Notes section are rewritten for the tabs.
AccessibilityEach year is <h3> wrapping the toggle button, each list a <ul> labelled by it. Selected row keeps aria-current. ◇ is decorative (aria-hidden). Contrast of faint text on page background: 4.9:1 light, 5.9:1 dark.
